kavier-energy — efficiency

Combine a Kavier tasks.parquet with OpenDC's powerSource.parquet into three per-million-token efficiency metrics. (Also available as the alias kavier-eff.)

This is a post-processing step: run kavier-perf, feed its OpenDC-compatible export through OpenDC to get a powerSource.parquet, then join the two here. All metrics are lower-is-better and reported per million tokens.

Flags & options

Flag / OptionTypeDefaultDescription
--kavierpathrequiredPath to tasks.parquet from kavier-perf (must keep its total_tokens column).
--opendcpathrequiredPath to powerSource.parquet from the matching OpenDC run.
--pricefloat(unset)GPU-hour price. No default — financial efficiency is reported only when you set it.
--outFILE.json(unset)If set, also writes the summary as JSON.

Metrics

KeyDefinition
energy_efficiency (Wh/Mtoken)Total energy (OpenDC, J→Wh) ÷ tokens, ×106.
carbon_efficiency (gCO2/Mtoken)Total CO2 (OpenDC, grams) ÷ tokens, ×106.
financial_efficiency ($/Mtoken)GPU-hours × --price ÷ tokens, ×106. null unless --price is given.
total_tokensSummed from the trace.

Example: smallest (no price → no cost metric)

kavier-energy \
  --kavier results/run-01/tasks.parquet \
  --opendc opendc/run-01/powerSource.parquet

Example: full

kavier-energy \
  --kavier results/run-01/tasks.parquet \
  --opendc opendc/run-01/powerSource.parquet \
  --price  12.0 \
  --out    results/run-01/efficiency_summary.json
